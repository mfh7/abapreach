Lists the objects that use one object, through ADT or the standard cross-reference tables

Usage: abapreach where-used [OPTIONS] --conn <CONN> <TYPE> <NAME>

Arguments:
  <TYPE>  PROG, FUNC, FUGR, CLAS, INTF, DTEL, DOMA, TABL or TTYP
  <NAME>  The object name

Options:
      --max <MAX>    1 to 5000 objects [default: 200]
      --conn <CONN>  
  -h, --help         Print help
