Deletes objects from a plan written by `--simulate`

Usage: abapreach delete [OPTIONS] --plan <PLAN> --conn <CONN> <TYPE:NAME>...

Arguments:
  <TYPE:NAME>...  

Options:
      --simulate     For DTEL, DOMA, TABL, TTYP and TRAN a simulate still creates and deletes a temporary class in $TMP
      --tr <TR>      
      --plan <PLAN>  
      --conn <CONN>  
  -h, --help         Print help
